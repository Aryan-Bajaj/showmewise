# Changelog

All notable changes to VideoDoc, version by version. Newest first.

## v5 (current)

The manual release: the clicks marked on the screen, in documents that read like a real process manual.

* **Marked screenshots.** Each action is pinned to its exact button or field with a numbered marker, using the OCR word positions and the narration timing. No marker is ever guessed.
* **Reads buttons and popups.** Short how-tos are read in full HD, and coloured buttons are found and read one by one.
* **Markers in clips and GIFs**, taken down as soon as the screen changes.
* **Process manual exports.** PDF, Word and HTML share one structure: cover, contents, purpose, what you need, the process at a glance, Part A, B, C with numbered actions and marked screenshots, result and tip boxes, checklist, quick reference, FAQ, page numbers.
* **Vid Chat shows the step** it answered from, with its marked screenshot and a play button, plus an optional web search.
* **Simple editing** with plain fields and labelled buttons.
* **Fixes.** The whole audio is transcribed (each window used to stop after 30 seconds), short videos split into several steps, and app names must appear on screen or in the audio. A screen error in one panel no longer blanks the page: the rest of the guide and the exports keep working.
* **Reads meeting recordings and dark screens.** A shared screen inside a meeting window is cut out from the margins and webcam tiles, so the text, the AI, the screenshots and the clips all see the app at full size. Dark-theme apps are read properly (light text on a dark background used to give almost nothing). A frame is taken about every 2 seconds in full HD (at 3 seconds, a window open for 3 seconds was missed).
* **Nothing important left out.** After each part is written, the codes, numbers, values, field names and actions that were said are checked against it; anything missing is asked for again by name. The AI also gets the screen text together with up to three pictures of each part.
* **Sturdier runs.** A dropped request to the local AI is tried again, and a part that still fails gets a second chance at the end. Every part that is left out says why in the browser console.
* **Honest progress.** No guessed run time: the time left is measured on your computer once writing starts. The transcription bar moves every few seconds and the download shows its size.
* **Accurate speech (optional).** Whisper large v3 turbo on the graphics card, for names, codes and mixed languages. If the graphics card cannot load it, the processor takes over by itself.
* **Long runs finish.** The page keeps the computer awake while a video is processed. The speech model is closed when the transcript is done, so its memory (up to 2 GB) goes back to the AI that writes the guide; a 16 GB laptop no longer swaps and each part is about 4 times faster. The AI gets a larger text window (8192 tokens), so long parts with three pictures are no longer refused.
* **No more graphics crash.** The animated app background kept the graphics card busy for hours while the page only waited, and on a full laptop that ended in a Windows crash. The app now uses a still background (0 % graphics load while waiting, measured).
* **Cleaner steps.** Folders of a menu tree read off the screen ("Expand Favorites") are no longer written as actions, and tips that only say something is irrelevant are dropped. A clear value on screen is never rewritten. The same action written twice (also in other words, or a plain line repeated later in the part) is kept once. Lines about what the trainer talks about ("Mention the option to save as") become the action itself ("Save as"), and remarks ("Acknowledge that this is test data") become the tip. Speech copied as a step ("You can save this version") becomes the order ("Save this version"); lines where the speaker talks about themselves, long menu-tree read-outs, misread screen labels, spoken sentences taken as typed values, and fields or option lists only read off the screen ("Identify the Ledger field", "View the options: Home, Reports, ...") are left out. Descriptions of the screen ("The dashboard displays a table") are left out when a part has real actions, and "If you want to add your filters, you can add" becomes "Add your filters".
* **Silent clicks.** Windows, tabs and fields that appear on screen while nobody speaks are listed in time order for the AI, so a click that is only shown (not said) can become a step. Before, only the first part of the screen text of a long part reached the AI. A window that is open for only a moment (a short SAP dialog) now counts too.
* **Key words box.** Under the upload, an optional field: names or codes from the video, separated by commas ("QX21N, Weekly Overview"). They fix how the speech was heard ("QX211N" becomes QX21N), are spelled exactly like this in the guide, and a part where one is said or shown must use it.
* **Said in the video, but not in this step.** Under a step, what was said (and is on screen) but is missing from it, with + Add and Hide.
* **Review mode.** The top of the guide says how many steps need a quick look; Show only these lists just those.
* **Test my computer.** Finds the browser AI size that runs well on this computer: tries Small, Medium and Large one by one, shows speed and a test answer, keeps the best and deletes the rest. With the plugin it also shows free memory and open tabs. After a failed load only smaller models are tried (a bigger one could freeze a laptop with built-in graphics).
* **Browser plugin (Chrome and Edge).** A small window under the toolbar icon: record any app (whole screen, a window or a tab) and the guide writes itself, the steps and time left, a notification when it is ready, and Vidi to ask about your video, the page you are on, a document or VideoDoc. Vidi reads matching links when the answer is not on the page, and Show me on the page marks the right button and adds a screenshot. The plugin uses the website as its engine, so models and saved progress are shared.
* **Record your screen on the website.** The whole screen, a window or a tab, with your voice. The recording is saved while it runs, so a crash does not lose it.
* **Step times leave out sleep.** A run that slept no longer shows 356 minutes; the time the computer slept is shown on its own.
* **PDF in Doc Chat, documents in Vid Chat.** Doc Chat now reads PDF files (with pdf.js, on your computer; scanned pages have no text to read). Vid Chat has Add a document: a manual, notes or a PDF is searched together with the video.
* **Vid Chat shows where to click.** An answer comes with the marked screenshot of each step it names, with only those markers drawn (up to three pictures, also when the answer runs over two parts). A question about something the guide never mentions ("How do I print it?") now says so plainly, instead of listing the first steps, and shows no picture.
* **Your pet in the plugin (BETA).** Vidi can live at the edge of your web pages as a small animated pet: a dragon, wolf, cat, fox or owl in three colours, your own picture, or your own 3D pet (.glb). It moves away from buttons and text by itself, hides half of itself when nobody needs it, sleeps when you are away, and you can drag it to either side. Click it to chat: about the page, about VideoDoc, or anything else, with web search answers from Wikipedia and DuckDuckGo with their links. It sets timers, alarms and reminders (a chime and a notification, while the browser is open), remembers your name and what you like, can be renamed, and asks now and then if it helped. Its chat and memory stay in this browser.
* **Ollama cloud models (optional).** For more reasoning power, Ollama's cloud models can be turned on in the AI settings, after a notice and an "I understand" tick: what such a model reads goes to Ollama's servers. Off by default, never picked by itself, clearly marked, with plain messages when you are not signed in, the free limit is reached or there is no internet.
* **Your pet guides you through a tool (BETA).** Give it the tool's manual and ask in your own words. It works out the task (and asks when it cannot tell), says where you are and gives one step at a time, marked on the page with a picture; you do each step and press Done, next step. It never clicks. In our test with a made-up tool and manual it was right in every check with the quick local model (gemma3:4b, 2 runs) and the free cloud model (gemma4:31b-cloud).
* **Accuracy next to each AI.** The pet's "change the AI" list shows how often each model was right in our own tests, with a note that results can differ.
* **Meet your pet.** The Pet training page starts with a few easy questions: your name, your pet and its colour, its name, the language and the AI (browser AI, Ollama on your computer, or Ollama cloud, each with its tested accuracy and speed). The pet is set up in the plugin in one click.
* **Ollama in three steps.** When Ollama is missing, VideoDoc shows one step at a time and ticks each one by itself: download Ollama for your system, let this website use it (one line to paste), and get the first model with a progress bar. With the cloud model, a small local backup that fits your computer's memory is offered.
* **A hello when the browser opens.** The pet pops out once with a playful greeting for the time of day, in your language.
* **Resize the chat.** Drag the corner of the pet's chat to make it bigger or smaller; it keeps that size. It never gets smaller than 260 by 300 points: closing it quickly right after a change once saved a size of 0, which put New chat and Close off the screen.
* **Hinglish stays in Latin letters.** A Hinglish question gets a Hinglish answer in Latin letters ("chalo, suno ..."). When the AI answers in Hindi letters anyway, the pet asks once more, then writes it in Latin letters itself.
* **One tap ideas.** Under the pet's chat, buttons are always there: Guide me on this tool, Help with my manual, Explain this page, Fill this page, Set a timer, Search the web.
* **The right button when there are two.** When a page has the same button twice (Delete next to Gift ideas and next to Shopping list), the pet marks the one you asked about. If it cannot tell, it marks each one and asks which you mean. A field and its own label are one thing: a single "Board name" field no longer asks "which one?" with two identical choices.
* **Help in the middle of a task.** "I typed the title but there is no save button" now goes on from the step you are at (write the text, then Save note), even when the AI words the steps differently or only repeats one you already did. Before, it started again at step 1.
* **The small browser AI guides better.** When it writes a step as a box of data, its text is used (it showed "[object Object]"), and when a button on the page fits your words better than its first step ("New board" for "create a board", not "Search boards"), the button is used. Its name in front of an answer ("Vidi: Vidi: ...") and any piece of the instructions it writes on after the answer are cut.
* **What it remembers, out in the open.** Your pet keeps your name, what you like, the file you gave it and saved form details, also after the browser or computer restarts. “what do you know about me?” lists all of it, “show my CV” names the file and the day you gave it, and 📄 Open shows the file in the plugin's own page (a PDF in the viewer, a web page without running any of its scripts, Word as a download), with Forget this file. Before filling a form it says which CV it uses and when you gave it, so an old CV is easy to replace.
* **Pet training as a course.** The page opens with a setup that starts by itself, one easy step per screen: pick the pet and its colour, name it, a little about you, then the AI with a 🧪 Test it button that checks right away that it runs on this computer (a real short answer, timed), and a backup AI for offline use after a cloud or browser AI. Then the lessons are cards, each with a picture of your own pet doing it, a name and two lines; one opens as its own scenario with the files to download, a short walkthrough with the words to type, and a place to try it. Done lessons turn green with ✓ Done and are kept; ↺ Reset starts over. Nothing is locked.
* **▶ Try it now.** In every lesson the words to say are buttons: one tap opens the pet's chat and says it for you, so you see it work at once. Each lesson says what it does and why in two short lines.
* **“Not in the document” is always clear.** When the AI says in its own words that the text does not cover a question, Doc Chat and Vid Chat now offer the two choices (the AI's own knowledge, or a web search) instead of showing it as a normal answer.
* **AIs as buttons, for this computer.** Ask your pet “change the AI” or “test my system”, or ask Vidi on the website “Which model should I pick?”: the AIs come as buttons, each with its size, how big or quick it is and how often it was right in our own tests (with a note that yours can differ), tagged ✅ Suggested or ⚠️ Not suggested for this computer's memory, suggested ones first and the most accurate on top. The ones not on the computer yet download with one tap and a progress bar, then are used at once. Without Ollama, the setup steps come first.
* **Your pet knows its own training.** Ask it “how do I give you another colour?” or “how do I make the chat bigger?” and it answers with the steps from the Pet training.
* **“What can you do?”** gets a short, happy answer with its name and skills, in the language you wrote in, and a button to the Pet training to try each one.
* **Every greeting says how to start** (“Just click me”), and an emptied name box in the plugin shows the pet's name again instead of staying blank.
* **Vid Chat and Doc Chat understand more.** One wrong letter in a longer word still finds it (“widgit” finds Add widget), the answer comes in the language of the question (Hinglish, German), “What is in this file?” gets a summary instead of “not found”, and each source part is listed once.
* **No lost clicks in a spoken sentence.** “Click Share, switch on anyone with the link, and copy the link” said in one breath is now three actions, so a part can no longer keep only “Click Share”.
* **Content Security Policy.** The website now runs only its own scripts and talks only to Ollama and the desktop engine on your computer, the AI model hosts and the two search sites. Text from a video, a document or a web page can never run as code; tested with attack text in every box, a page with hidden orders for the AI, and a page pretending to be the VideoDoc site.
* **Says what it cannot do.** Asked to open an app or send an email, the pet says it cannot and offers to help with the text, in your language, even when the AI starts to pretend.
* **Vid Chat finds the part by its title.** "How do I change the title of the chart?" now answers from the part called Edit title, not from the chart part whose screen also shows the word Title.
* **Short answers.** The pet answers in a sentence or two unless you ask for details, and explains a page in its own words instead of copying it.
* **Easier to read.** The Training link in the pet is a lighter blue, and the Pet training page uses the website's look, with lessons for guiding and for same name buttons.
* **Limits told up front.** The website, the How to use page and Vidi now say what VideoDoc cannot do yet, with the test results (60 to 67.5 % of actions fully right with the best setup). Vidi also explains the progress list: why a job says Skipped, what "kept from the last try" means, and why a run that slept shows a long time.
* **Picks up where it stopped.** The transcript, the screen text and every part already written are kept in this browser while a video runs. After a crash, a closed tab or sleep, the same video with the same settings continues instead of starting over.
* **No made-up product names.** Purpose, what you need, summary and FAQ only name apps that are said or shown in the video; any other name becomes "the app" (a demo of a made-up app once came back as "Access to Grafana").
* **Cleaner HTML guide.** The zoomed crop now only appears in a part without marked screenshots; next to the numbered markers its loose box added nothing.
* **See an example guide.** A finished guide made from the public demo video, one click from the landing page.
* **Flow diagram you can edit.** The diagram follows the steps (rename, move or delete a step and it redraws). In Edit document every box and phase can be renamed, added or removed; "Rebuild from steps" brings back the automatic one. The exports use what you see.
* **Clicks nobody says.** When a window opens, the mouse pointer in the frame just before shows what was clicked: the word under it, or the toolbar icon that opened the window. That click is always in the guide, also when the AI left it out. No pointer found means nothing is added. It finds the pointer on dark screens (white arrow) and on light pages (the arrow's dark edge). When no window opens, a click is found where the pointer rests on a word while the area around it changes (a tick box, a tab, a row); those go on the part's checklist, never straight into the guide.
* **The whole transcript first.** Before the parts are written, the AI reads the full transcript once and lists every action with its time. Each one is checked against what was said at that time, and only joins its part when one of its names is on screen there (or it carries a code or number), so an action said in passing is not lost and loose talk does not become a step.
* **Noise removal tested, not added.** A background-noise remover was built and measured on the same speech with light to very heavy noise added. Whisper made as few or fewer mistakes without it (0 to 1.7 % word errors) than with it (up to 4.3 %), so the sound keeps only the light speech filter it already had.
* **Measured, then decided.** Two long test runs judged against hand-written manuals: Self-verify removed correct details and added 20 to 30 minutes, so it is now off by default. Consolidate no longer moves a part to an earlier place in the guide (it only merges neighbours). The blur effects are gone (they kept the graphics card at 40 to 77 % while processing), which also made speech recognition about 30 % faster. Run-time notes now quote the measured times.
* **How to use page.** Every screen and every setting explained in plain English with real screenshots and numbered pins, plus all error messages with what to do.
* **Vidi, the help chat.** A help button at the bottom right of every page. It answers from the How to use text, knows which page you are on, notices error messages by itself and offers help (Yes / No), and reads pasted screenshots on your computer. Short answers unless you ask for details. Works without any AI, with Ollama, or with a small browser AI (Qwen2.5 1.5B: in a test with Vidi's own questions it answered 83 % right, the smaller Llama 3.2 1B only 33 %).
* **Ollama found, Ollama used.** When Ollama runs, the settings and Doc Chat choose it with its best (or quickest) model. Cloud models are never offered.
* **Harder to break.** A file that is not a video gets a clear message, a video without sound is documented from the screen, New video starts over after an error, closing the tab mid-run asks first, two buttons that did nothing were removed, edit boxes grow with their text.
* **Better chat answers.** Vid Chat now searches the written steps (it used to search only the transcript), finds exact words as well as meaning, answers order questions (first, after, last) from the right part, skips the summary sections of a guide saved by VideoDoc (they mixed other parts into an answer), and shows lists and bold text cleanly. Text in a video or document can not give the AI orders.
* **Site.** New logo, privacy and terms page, share previews, sitemap, security headers, updated dependencies. On a phone, a notice says up front that VideoDoc is made for a computer and how much it would download. The in-browser model sizes on the site now match the app. Every sentence on the home page was checked against the app: no quality percentage that was never measured, no "done in minutes", honest speech accuracy, and the roadmap no longer lists languages that already work. On a phone the top bar and the size strip fit in one line, the phone notice is no longer covered by the help button, and the How to use pictures show their numbers when tapped (large, swipe sideways).

## v4

Accuracy release, driven by testing against a real 32 minute screen recording of a business app and checking every generated step against the actual video.

* **Structured output.** The model replies in schema constrained JSON on every engine (Ollama, llama.cpp, WebLLM), so malformed or chatty replies are impossible. The schema forces the model to describe what it sees on screen before it may decide a segment has no step, which fixed the biggest source of missed steps in real meeting recordings.
* **Screen share detection.** Webcam only minutes at the start of a meeting can no longer produce fabricated steps.
* **Scene based steps.** Step boundaries follow pixel level screen changes instead of a fixed clock, so steps line up with what actually happened.
* **Recall guard.** A skipped segment with real evidence (spoken action words or a busy screen) gets a second look before it is allowed to vanish.
* **Grounded summaries.** The executive summary no longer invents decisions, outcomes or next steps to round off the story, and never names software that was not on screen.
* **Cleaner input.** Meeting chatter, filler and speech recognition repeats are scrubbed from the transcript before the model sees it. Garbled OCR identifiers are stripped from results.
* **Editing.** Steps can now be reordered and removed, not just edited, and every change flows into the exports.
* **Two builds.** A hosted web build with per video size and length limits, and a fully offline desktop build that bundles llama.cpp with the gemma 3 vision model (see desktop/README.md).

## v3 — Sees and hears, like a person

The biggest leap yet. The AI now watches the screen and hears every word, so a recording becomes a real Desktop Procedure you can follow without opening the video.

* **Vision.** The AI sees the actual screenshots (gemma3 via Ollama, or Phi-3.5-Vision in the browser), not just OCR text. It names exact buttons and works out "click here" by looking.
* **Whisper Small.** A far more accurate transcript: names, accents and technical jargon.
* **A real Desktop Procedure.** Index, Purpose, Prerequisites, numbered steps (action, result, note, screen capture), Key Observations, Executive Summary, FAQ, and a flow diagram.
* **Sharp WebM step clips.** True colour, clearer and smaller than GIFs, inlined into the self-contained HTML.
* **Smarter steps.** One clean step per segment, off-topic and silent stretches skipped, duplicates merged. A one-hour video reads as roughly 30 to 35 steps, not 48 fragments.
* **Steps say WHERE.** Each action names the app, ribbon, tab or menu, not just the element.
* **RAG-grounded FAQ.** Diverse questions from the discussion, each answer retrieved from the transcript and enriched with the model's general knowledge, without inventing specifics.
* **Clean phased flow diagram**, **hidden transcript for AI tools**, **stronger model picker**, **faster and safer pipeline** (cancel, progress bar, no stalls), and **domain mishearing fixes**.

## v2 — Eyes, motion, and chat

* **OCR** reads on-screen text; tools (Excel, SAP, VBA and more) are auto-detected.
* **Animated step GIFs** of the real action.
* **Vid Chat (RAG):** ask your recording questions, with grounded answers and the matching frame.
* **Chunking** for long videos, with whole-video coverage.
* **You name the guide**, a browser-first **model picker**, and a standalone **Doc Chat** app.

## v1 — The basics

* Transcribe a recording and grab frames every few seconds.
* One LLM call per segment to write the steps.
* A few static screenshots per step.
* Export to HTML.
