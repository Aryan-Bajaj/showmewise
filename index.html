VideoDoc

Turn screen recordings into step-by-step guides, with the clicks marked on the screen.

Record your screen once. VideoDoc listens to what you say, reads the text on screen and looks at every step, then writes a finished guide: a cover, the purpose, what you need, the process at a glance, numbered steps with marked screenshots, a checklist and FAQ. Export it as HTML, PDF or Word.

It is free, needs no account and no API key, and runs on your own computer. Your video is never uploaded.
