Demo Planner manual

Create a new board

  1. Click the New board button.
  2. Type a name in the Board name field.
  3. Optional: pick a Colour.
  4. Click Create board. The new board appears in the list.

Share a board

  1. Click Share next to the board.
  2. Type an e-mail address in the Share with field.
  3. Click Send.
